Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: conceptual explanatory illustration for slide 12 of a knowledge-work course, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: show that many streams of work needing judgment converge on a small number of human reviewers. Make the many-to-few relationship instantly understandable at thumbnail size.
Style/medium: minimal flat editorial illustration; warm ivory background, muted warm gray, slate blue, and restrained terracotta red accents. Solid color shapes, clean uniform lines, no texture, no dimensional lighting or shadows. Calm, generous spacing.
Composition: upper quarter mostly open for a separately added slide title. Across the middle and lower center, five small slate-blue document cards form a spacious vertical column on the left. Each card has only two short straight uniform strokes, never handwriting. Five clearly traceable smooth connector paths flow from those cards towards only two simple human bust silhouettes on the right, three paths to the upper reviewer and two to the lower reviewer. Separate the two sets cleanly without crossing paths. The paths terminate in a small terracotta pending-work card beside each reviewer, with a visible gap before the silhouette so no line touches or passes through a person. The narrowing spacing near these two destinations communicates concentration of review work. Humans have a round featureless head and one simple shoulder shape, no clothing details. Give the two destinations generous vertical separation.
Visual priority: the five-to-two flow is the single main idea. Reviewer silhouettes are readable and slightly larger than the cards; document details stay subordinate. This is a conceptual illustration, not a data chart.
Text: no words, labels, numbers, logos, or watermark.
Avoid: photorealism, 3D, gradients, drop shadows, paper textures, scribbles, fake writing, complex faces, hair details, office scenery, desks, plants, decorative icons, circuitry, dense tangles, excessive details, alarming or punitive imagery.
